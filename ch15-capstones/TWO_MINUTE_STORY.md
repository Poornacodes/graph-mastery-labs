# The two-minute story (template)

Recruiters and hiring managers buy stories, not feature lists. Fill this in
for each capstone, then rehearse it out loud until it fits in two minutes.

1. **Problem (20 seconds).** Who had the problem, and why did it matter?
   *"Support engineers at a 300-person company spent hours finding which
   service owned a failing component..."*
2. **Why a graph (15 seconds).** What about the problem is relational or
   multi-hop?
3. **The model (25 seconds).** The three or four node types and the key
   relationships, and the one modeling decision you are proudest of.
4. **The key query or algorithm (25 seconds).** The traversal, algorithm, or
   GraphRAG step that does the work, in one sentence.
5. **The result (20 seconds).** A number: accuracy versus a baseline, latency,
   hours saved, rings found.
6. **What you would do next (15 seconds).** One honest limitation and how you
   would address it.

Keep a version with the numbers on one index card.
